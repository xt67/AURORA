# AURORA working-paper guidelines

Use the supplied generic article template, not a conference acceptance style.
Author: Rayan Rahman. Affiliation: Marathwada Mitra Mandal Institute of Technology.
Write a protocol and execution-feasibility report. Only one two-epoch run exists.
Keep future publication experiments separate and in future tense. Do not invent
classifier metrics, quality scores, error bars, clinical review, ethics approval,
patient identifiers, privacy guarantees or novelty. Negative findings are valid.
Use only supplied figures; never synthesize medical evidence or extrapolate losses.
Do not describe the 590-image subset as confirmed duplicate-free.
Reference verified primary sources; use references.bib and plainnat.
Preserve author-confirmation placeholders for declarations. Label the paper a draft.
Disclose AI writing assistance. Do not attribute authorship to an AI tool.
